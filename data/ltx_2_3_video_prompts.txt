=== SCENE 01 | 6.0 | Thrown awake ===
A 35mm camera descends from three hanging lanterns through the ceiling beams to Shen Sheng lying face-down in water. A sailor enters from the right, grips Shen Sheng's collar, lifts him, then throws him back down. The lantern light crosses the sailor's cheek as he moves. Shen Sheng presses his right hand into the water. The camera ends on ripples spreading from his hand. Boot splashes, water drops, hull creaks, and waves are audible.

=== SCENE 02 | 6.0 | Wake and humiliation ===
From Shen Sheng's right hand at water level, a 40mm camera tracks backward with the ripples as he pushes up and coughs water, wet hair covering his eyes. A sailor crosses the foreground, briefly blocking the light on Shen Sheng's cheek. The sailor says in English, "Yellow rat, tell me if you cannot stand. I will kill you quickly." He laughs and walks three splashing steps away. Shen Sheng raises only his pupils toward the sailor's back. The camera ends on Shen Sheng's eyes. Splashes, coughs, drips, and one low cello note are audible.

=== SCENE 03 | 7.0 | Memory cut ===
An 85mm camera pushes from Shen Sheng's wet cheek to his closing eye. On a heartbeat, hard cut to an orphanage gate; hard cut to hands on a keyboard; hard cut to a neon city; hard cut to a young man on an ochre medieval street; hard cut to a pale hand with blood beside a hospital bed. White flashes separate the cuts. End on a dark pupil filling the frame. Drips change into keyboard clicks, footsteps, coughs, and one electronic pulse.

=== SCENE 04 | 6.0 | Recognition ===
From black, a 100mm camera holds Shen Sheng's pupil as a lantern flame moves in its reflection, then pulls back while he opens his eyes and wipes his cheek. His mouth corners lift briefly, then close. The camera follows his hand down to wet planks and ends on three fingertip taps. A water drop, a hull creak, three taps, then a low cello note are audible.

=== SCENE 05 | 7.0 | Looking around ===
Starting on Shen Sheng's tapping fingers, a 50mm camera rises and circles clockwise. His eyes move from Michael to the woman holding a Bible and then to George. A lantern stripe crosses each face. A small blue reflection appears once in the puddle and passes over Shen Sheng's knuckles. George draws a breath, stands, and raises one hand. The camera stops on George's hand. Finger taps, water, murmurs, and cello continue.

=== SCENE 06 | 7.0 | George speaks ===
A handheld 50mm camera moves backward as George stands, points ahead, swallows, looks behind him, and points again. He says in English, "There is a ghost. Little Tom died when old John came back. Blue and violet. It smiled. Tom died." His hand opens and closes after each phrase. People step back. The woman raises her Bible, and the camera follows it upward. Water drips and a bowed string note continue.

=== SCENE 07 | 7.0 | The Bible and the crowd ===
A 50mm camera follows the raised Bible to the woman's face, then pans to a thin man by a timber post. The woman looks toward the stairs and says, "Father Evan is a priest from Coman Town. The church will protect us." The man folds his arms and answers, "A priest cannot save you from that." Three people behind them say, "Blue-violet," "It kills," and "The priest will help us." George turns his head upward. Chains, water, voices, and a low string note build.

=== SCENE 08 | 6.0 | Gunshots ===
Hard cut to a locked 24mm wide shot. Four gunshots sound above, followed by two screams. George freezes with one hand raised, the Bible falls, the thin man turns, and Shen Sheng bends his knees and raises a hand. The lantern chains stop. One water drop lands in a puddle after the echoes. The camera ends on its ripple; all music has stopped.

=== SCENE 09 | 7.0 | The stairs ===
A 50mm camera looks over Shen Sheng's shoulder and racks focus from his wet hair to the stairwell. George lowers his hand and says, "Could it be the ghost?" A blue reflection crosses Shen Sheng's cheek and disappears. Shen Sheng rises from one knee while watching the stairs. The camera pans left as Michael enters the stairwell light. A single water drop repeats with no music.

=== SCENE 10 | 7.0 | Michael climbs ===
A low 35mm camera looks up as Michael grips a ladder rung, looks toward the opening, and says, "No. I am not mad. It is too cramped in here." He takes one breath and climbs two rungs while the camera rises beneath him. His body blocks the light above and sends a shadow down the ladder. Two migrants climb behind him. The camera ends on his boot pressing a plank. Breathing, boots, water, wood creaks, and one cello note are audible.

=== SCENE 11 | 5.0 | Fall ===
Match cut from Michael's boot to a high 28mm view. A tearing sound begins; Michael and two migrants fall straight down. The camera drops briefly, then stops above the floor as three bodies strike. Michael looks upward and exhales white breath. Tearing wood, impacts, and crying replace the cello. The camera ends on the breath cloud.

=== SCENE 12 | 8.0 | Cold light ===
From Michael's white breath at floor level, a 40mm camera slides toward the people at the wall. Each exhale adds fog; ice crystals spread over wet boards. Blue-violet light enters through ceiling cracks and covers the amber pools one by one. People cover their mouths and step back. The camera tilts up with widening violet light. Breath, hum, bow scrape, ice cracks, water, then a wordless choir are audible.

=== SCENE 13 | 10.0 | Ghost down ===
From a ceiling crack, a low 28mm camera tilts back as a blue-violet ghost comes through the wood. Its lower body changes into smoke, bone spurs extend from its arms, and violet light moves inside empty eye sockets. The violet light changes faces, water, and wood. People step back, kneel, or cover their heads. Shen Sheng presses against the wall and looks up. The ghost turns toward Shen Sheng until violet fills the frame. Hum, ice, choir, and breathing continue.

=== SCENE 14 | 7.0 | Ninety-nine ===
Match cut from violet light to a 100mm close-up of Shen Sheng's eye, then pull back. Violet light covers one cheek and amber light covers the other. A red pulse without letters moves in his eye; the choir stops. A synthesized voice says, "Connection. Ninety-nine percent. Load failed. Reconnecting." Shen Sheng looks toward the ghost, swallows, opens and closes his mouth, then turns his head left. The camera pushes in with one alarm beep.

=== SCENE 15 | 6.0 | A ghost ===
A 35mm over-the-shoulder shot starts behind Shen Sheng and moves toward the ghost. Cold fog crosses Shen Sheng's shoulder, bone spurs extend, and lantern flames go out one by one. Shen Sheng reaches the wall, exhales once, and says, "There really is a ghost." An alarm beep, hum, choir, and heartbeat play together; the choir, alarm, and hum stop in sequence. The camera holds his side profile and cuts to black on one heartbeat.
