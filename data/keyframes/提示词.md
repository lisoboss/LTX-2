# 《深空》第一集：逐时点 I2V 关键帧图片提示词

一张图片只代表**一个准确时点**。`first.png` 必须是视频第 0 帧；不能把稍后才出现的人物、动作结果或光色变化放进首帧。每个镜头的 `middle_XX.png` 与 `last.png` 都是可选的，但只要希望 LTX 精确经过该状态，就应生成并在 `keyframes.ini` 中写入对应秒点。

所有图片为 **1280×768、5:3 横构图**。每条英文画面描述前均添加这段固定前缀：

```text
Photorealistic dark historical fantasy drama, 35mm film grain, 5:3 landscape, no readable text, no subtitles, no watermark. In the wooden lower hold of a smuggler ship: wet brown planks, dirty water, three amber kerosene lanterns, deep brown shadows. Shen Sheng is a lean young Asian man in his twenties, high cheekbones, wet black hair, pale dirty skin, soaked patched gray-brown tunic. Michael is a muscular Black man in his thirties wearing a white sleeveless undershirt and dark work trousers. George is a gaunt middle-aged white man with greasy gray-brown hair, bloodshot blue eyes, and a torn faded wool sweater. The woman is a middle-aged Black woman in a faded orange-yellow headwrap, navy dress, pale blouse, holding a worn leather Bible.
```
